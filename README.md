## Featured Project: Unified Business Regulatory Platform

### 🏛️ [Unified Business Regulatory Platform](./unified-business-platform/)
A comprehensive Figma prototype for streamlining government regulatory processes for businesses in India.

**Problem Solved**: Fragmented regulatory processes across multiple government departments, slow approvals, and lack of centralized tracking.

**Key Features**:
- Unified Business Dashboard
- AI-Powered Guidance System  
- Centralized Application Tracking
- Digital Document Locker
- Multi-language Support (Hindi, English, Regional)
- WCAG 2.1 AA Accessibility Compliance

**Deliverables**:
